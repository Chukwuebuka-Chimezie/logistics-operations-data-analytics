# Logistics Operations Data Analytics

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of logistics operations using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on operational performance, revenue, service reliability, fleet activity, fuel efficiency, safety, and customer-related performance.

The project demonstrates how raw operational data can be transformed into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data modelling, KPI development, PivotTables, and interactive dashboard reporting.

## Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer key logistics business questions, including:

- How is overall logistics performance changing over time?
- How much revenue is being generated from loads?
- How reliable are pickup and delivery operations?
- Where are the major service bottlenecks and detention hotspots?
- How efficiently are vehicles and drivers being utilized?
- What operational risks require management attention?
- Which areas can be improved to support better service and operational efficiency?

## Data Modelling

A relational data model was developed using Power Pivot. Key relationships were established between operational tables to enable analysis across customers, loads, trips, drivers, trucks, routes, fuel, maintenance, delivery events, and safety incidents.

The model was designed to support reliable filtering, aggregation, and cross-functional logistics analysis.

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several important operational patterns:

- The dataset contains 85,410 loads and generates approximately $262.5 million in recorded revenue.
- Delivery operations have a lower on-time performance than pickup operations.
- Detention is a significant operational issue, with delivery events accounting for a larger share of total detention time than pickup events.
- Several cities and states show consistently high detention levels, indicating potential service bottlenecks.
- Safety incidents provide additional insight into operational risk, preventability, injuries, and claims.
- Revenue, service reliability, detention, fleet activity, and safety can be analyzed together to support better operational decision-making.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management should consider:

- Investigating locations with consistently high detention times.
- Improving delivery scheduling and operational coordination.
- Monitoring service reliability separately for pickup and delivery activities.
- Using fleet and driver performance metrics to identify efficiency opportunities.
- Strengthening safety monitoring and preventive controls.
- Using KPI-driven dashboards for continuous operational performance management.
